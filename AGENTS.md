behavior, countdowns, black-market orders, storage, or end-of-day logic, read:

`Doc/GameplayLoopContract.md`

Treat its Beginning of Day, During the Day, and End of Day sections as the
source of truth. Update that document whenever an API, event, owner, or phase
responsibility changes.

current behavior and unfinished integration points.
